# Designüberarbeitung V2

Eigene Art Direction mit warmem Off-White, dunklem Tannengrün, sanftem Limettengrün und Korallorange. Die Bogenfigur steht als visuelle Metapher für das Verbinden von Systemen. Sie ist kein echter KI-Assistent und hat keine Chatfunktion. Keine fremde Figur oder Kundenreferenz.

Umgesetzt: neue Startseite, klickbare Anwendungsbeispiele, differenzierte Leistungsdarstellung, anschauliche Beispielseiten, überarbeitete Lösungen, gemeinsame Bildsprache und stärkerer Kontaktabschluss. Die Formulare und Backendlogik bleiben erhalten. Bewegungen werden bei `prefers-reduced-motion` deaktiviert.

## Bildherstellung

Erstellt mit dem eingebauten Imagegen-Werkzeug (kein CLI/API-Fallback). Originale als PNG lokal gespeichert und als komprimierte WebP-Dateien mit Transparenz für die Website optimiert. Die Bilder werden mit Next Image responsiv ausgeliefert. Es sind konzeptionelle Illustrationen, keine Fotos eines echten Teams oder echte Software-Screenshots.

## Finaler Prompt: Maskottchen

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent 3D mascot cutout for an original premium German B2B process automation website. Primary request: create an original approachable, quietly confident little process connector mascot, a sculptural warm coral-orange arch-shaped creature. The body is one chunky rounded inverted U, a small architectural gateway, approximately the shape of a smooth lowercase n, with a solid rounded upper bridge and two short substantial legs. Two tiny dark pine-green oval eyes directly on the front of the upper arch, no screen, no mouth, not a conventional robot. Two small simple rounded arms; one hand gently holds a short dark pine-green cable with a pale lime rounded square connector, the other hand open in a helpful welcoming gesture. Three-quarter view facing slightly left. Tactile high-end matte ceramic / soft touch material, subtle fine grain, restrained charming proportions, physically plausible soft studio lighting and contact shading. Isolated full body with generous transparent padding, no platform and no environment, real transparent alpha. Composition: mascot occupies 80% of a square frame, all limbs and cable inside frame. Palette warm coral #f28b62, pine #163e37, muted soft lime #d9f38b. Intended use: hero visual against a pale sage panel and small reuse beside a contact CTA. Constraints: entirely original character, no known IP, no logos, no text, no watermark, no UI, no bubbles, no sparkles, no glossy toy plastic, not childish, no background or opaque floor.

## Finaler Prompt: Prozessillustration

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide editorial 3D illustration for a premium German B2B automation website, used as a large service-page image and process example illustration. Primary request: create a visually striking, very clean architectural still life representing disconnected business tools becoming one connected process. On a pale warm ivory studio floor are three substantial rounded rectangular blocks stepping across the composition: muted pastel sage block at left with a minimal inset mail-envelope geometric relief; a dark pine green low cuboid in center with a neat row of three small pale lime cylinders; a coral-orange rounded upright arch gateway at right. One elegant dark green cable winds from the first block through the center toward the arch, visibly connecting all three. A small pale lime connector rests in the foreground. No character faces in this image. Soft sophisticated ceramic and powder-coated material, subtle tactile texture, carefully composed afternoon studio light, crisp long soft shadows, restrained original art direction. Palette ivory #f4f5ed, pine #173d37, coral #f28b62, pale lime #d9f38b. Composition wide 3:2, three-quarter elevated camera, objects in lower two-thirds, plenty of calm space between objects, consistent scale, all objects completely inside frame. Constraints: no text, no logos, no numbers, no watermarks, no stock-photo humans, no neon, no glassmorphism, no overcomplicated machine, no gradient background. This is conceptual illustration, not a real product screenshot.
